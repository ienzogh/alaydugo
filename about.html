AlayDugo

"Alay" means offering; "dugo" means blood. AlayDugo is a community platform that makes the offering of blood easier to give and easier to find.

About

AlayDugo is a blood donation awareness website dedicated to encouraging people to donate blood and help individuals who need it.

Goal

Our goal is to connect people through kindness and promote the importance of voluntary blood donation. We believe that a simple act of giving can make a meaningful difference in someone's life.
